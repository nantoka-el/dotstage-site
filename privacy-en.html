DOT STAGE

Privacy Policy

日本語 (This English page is a translation. If the two differ, the Japanese page applies.)

DOT STAGE ("the app") is an iPhone app provided by Kosuke Nakayama (Kirara-san) ("the operator"). This page explains how the app handles information.

1. Information we collect

Through the app, the operator does not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, phone number, postal address, location, or contacts. There is no account sign-up. Information that reaches Google for ads and usage statistics is described in section 5.

2. Information kept only on your iPhone

  • Songs you import (audio files and the sound of videos): All song analysis (splitting into parts, counting beats, and so on) happens on your iPhone. Songs are never sent anywhere.
  • Characters you draw or make from pictures: Stored only on your iPhone.
  • Videos you make: Stored on your iPhone. They are saved to Photos or sent to other apps only when you choose to. How they are handled after that depends on the rules of the service you send them to.

All of these are removed from your iPhone when you delete the app. The operator cannot see them.

3. Permissions

  • Add to Photos: Used to save the videos you make to Photos.
  • Camera: Used to take a photo when you make a character from a picture.
  • Tracking: Used to show ads that suit you (section 5).

Everything else in the app works without these permissions. You can change them at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

4. Loading notices

To tell you about new versions and notices, the app reads https://dotstage.kirarasan.com/notice.json at most once a day. As with viewing any web page, the server it reads from (GitHub Pages) receives connection information such as your IP address. The operator does not collect this information or link it to you. The app only sends the request to read the file; it does not send songs, your works, or information about your device.

For how GitHub handles this, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5. Usage statistics (analytics) and ads

The app uses two Google services. Neither of them receives song titles or song contents, text you type, characters you draw, videos you make, or file names.

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): how far you got through the screens, the IDs of the cards and stages chosen, the type and length of songs, how long song analysis and video making took and whether they worked, how much memory was free, progress through the tutorials and gifts, whether an ad was shown, and whether you bought "Remove Ads" (Google Analytics also counts App Store purchases). Along with these, Google receives a number the app creates for each device (it changes if you reinstall the app), your device model, iOS version, app version, and approximate region (from your IP address). If you allow tracking on your iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is also sent. This is used to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): The app shows ads. To show them, Google receives device information, your IP address, and which ads you saw or tapped. If you allow tracking on your iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is also sent, and you see ads that suit you. The whole app works even if you don't allow tracking (you then see ads that are not personalized).
  • Recipient: Google LLC. For how Google handles this information, see the Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase, and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Payment for "Remove Ads" (an in-app purchase) is handled by Apple. The operator never receives your card or other payment details.

6. Children's information

The operator does not collect information that identifies users, whatever their age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
